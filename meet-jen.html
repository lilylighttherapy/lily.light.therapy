MEET JEN

Your Safe Space

Hi, I'm Jenefa Anthony, though most people call me Jen. I'm a Licensed and Registered Counsellor with the Lembaga Kaunselor Malaysia, the founder of Lily & Light Therapy Services, and someone who feels deeply about the importance of being understood.

One of the things I have come to appreciate through my work is that we rarely know the full story behind another person's smile.

Sometimes, the people who seem to have everything together are the ones quietly struggling the most. The ones who are always there for everyone else may not know who to turn to when they need someone. And sometimes, we become so accustomed to being strong, keeping the peace, and carrying on that we forget we are allowed to struggle, too.

Perhaps you know what that feels like.

Maybe you've been holding things in for a long time. Maybe there are experiences from your past that still affect you in ways you don't fully understand. Maybe you find yourself repeating the same patterns in your relationships, questioning your worth, or feeling overwhelmed by emotions you cannot quite explain.

Or perhaps, nothing looks particularly wrong from the outside. You just know that something inside you doesn't feel right anymore.

Whatever brings you here, you don't have to prove that your pain is serious enough to deserve support.

I didn't choose counselling because I believe I have all the answers. I chose this work because I believe there is something deeply meaningful about helping people understand themselves, make sense of their experiences, and discover that their struggles do not have to define their future.

As a counsellor with a Master's degree in Counselling Psychology from Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (UKM), I bring professional knowledge, evidence-informed therapeutic approaches, and a commitment to ethical, responsible practice into my work.

But beyond the qualifications and techniques, I believe in the importance of how a person feels in the room with me.

I want you to feel that you can speak honestly, even when your thoughts are messy or your feelings are difficult to explain. That you can take your time. That you don't have to tell me everything before you are ready. That you can disagree, ask questions, or admit that you don't know what you need.

I am not here to judge your choices, dictate how you should live, or turn you into someone you are not.

I am here to listen, to ask questions that help you look a little deeper, and to walk alongside you as we explore what you are experiencing and what meaningful change might look like for you.

My approach draws on different therapeutic modalities because I recognise that no two people have the same story. Our sessions are shaped around your experiences, needs, strengths, and goals. Sometimes, that means working through difficult emotions. Sometimes, it means recognising patterns you have never noticed before. Sometimes, it means learning to set boundaries, rebuilding trust in yourself, or making space for parts of your story you have never felt able to share.

And sometimes, progress begins with something as simple as finally saying out loud what you have been afraid to admit, even to yourself.

MY APPROACH

Gentle. Collaborative. Individualised.

Therapy is a relationship, not a formula. I begin by understanding you — your experiences, concerns, strengths and what you hope to change.

01

Listen

We begin by creating a safe space for you to share what brings you to counselling and what matters most to you.

02

Understand

Together, we explore your emotions, thoughts, experiences, relationships and patterns with curiosity and compassion.

03

Connect

We explore the connections between what you've experienced, how you feel, how you respond and what you need.

04

Move Forward

Together, we identify meaningful and practical steps that align with your needs, values and goals.

There is no need to rush the process. Healing can happen one step at a time.
