“Starting therapy felt pretty intimidating, especially since I didn't know what to expect, but Jen made the whole process so easy. “
“When I first started counselling, I was going through a difficult period in my life and felt unsure about what to do next. Having someone listen and help me organise my thoughts made a difference. Jen didn't simply tell me what decisions to make; she helped me explore my feelings, consider different perspectives and recognise what mattered to me. “I used to overthink almost everything, and honestly, it was exhausting. I would keep replaying conversations in my head and worry about things that hadn't even happened. I'm still learning, but I can see changes in how I handle situations now. I'm glad I decided to give therapy a chance.”
