HOW I CAN HELP

Therapy that meets you where you are.

Every person comes with a different story, experiences and needs. Therapy is tailored around you rather than following a one-size-fits-all approach.

01

Individual Counselling

A private and supportive space to explore your thoughts, emotions, experiences, relationships and personal goals.

02

Couples Counselling

A space for couples to better understand one another, work through relationship difficulties, improve communication and rebuild connection.

03

Family Counselling

A supportive space for families to improve communication, understand one another's perspectives, work through difficulties and strengthen relationships.

04

Personal Growth

Develop greater self-awareness, understand patterns, clarify your values, strengthen boundaries and move towards a life that feels more meaningful.

05

Psychological Assessments

Professional psychological assessment services to support greater understanding of psychological and emotional functioning, where appropriate.

06

Workplace Wellbeing

Mental health and wellbeing support designed for organisations, including workplace wellbeing initiatives and programmes.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Your first session doesn't have to be perfect.

You don't need to prepare a speech.

You don't need to know exactly what is wrong.

And you don't have to tell me everything in your first session.

Your first session is an opportunity for us to get to know one another, understand what brought you to counselling, explore what you hope to work on and discuss how therapy may support you. You can ask questions too.

We start where you are.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

If your question isn't answered here, you're welcome to reach out.

What is the duration of a session?

Individual counselling sessions are 60 minutes, while couples counselling sessions are 90 minutes.

Do you offer online counselling?

Yes. Online counselling is available via Google Meet for clients throughout Malaysia.

Where are physical sessions conducted?

In-person counselling is available in Bandar Sri Sendayan, Seremban, and Petaling Jaya.

Do I need to know what I want to talk about before therapy?

No. You don't need to have everything figured out before reaching out. We can begin by exploring what is currently happening and what brought you to counselling.

Is counselling confidential?

Counselling is confidential within the applicable professional, legal and safeguarding requirements. The limits of confidentiality will be explained clearly during the counselling process and informed consent.

What if I'm not sure whether therapy is right for me?

That's completely okay. You can reach out with your questions first. We can discuss what you're looking for and whether counselling may be appropriate for your needs.
